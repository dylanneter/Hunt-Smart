    How the 0–100 score is built

    Dawn & duskDeer move most just after sunrise and before sunset
    Solunar & moonMajor/minor periods, new and full moon
    Pressure & frontsRising pressure and cold snaps boost movement
    Wind & rainHigh wind and heavy rain slow deer down
